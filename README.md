## How to Install (Windows)

### Option A: Portable Version (No Installation Needed)

This is the simplest option — just download, extract, and run.

1. Your admin will share the **SynapseAi-Windows-v1.0.0.zip** file with you
2. Right-click the ZIP file and select **Extract All...**
3. Choose a location (e.g., your Desktop or Documents folder)
4. Click **Extract**
5. Open the extracted folder
6. Double-click **SynapseAi.exe** to launch the app

> **Tip:** Right-click **SynapseAi.exe** and select **Pin to taskbar** or **Create shortcut** for quick access.

### Option B: Installer Version

If your admin provides the **SynapseAi-Setup-1.0.0.exe** installer:

1. Double-click **SynapseAi-Setup-1.0.0.exe**
2. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click **More info** and then **Run anyway** (the app is safe — it's just not published to the Microsoft Store)
3. Follow the installation wizard:
   - Choose where to install (default is fine)
   - Click **Install**
4. Once installed, the app will appear in your Start menu and as a desktop shortcut
5. Double-click the shortcut to launch

## Windows SmartScreen Warning

Since this app is distributed directly (not through the Microsoft Store), Windows may show a security warning the first time you run it:

1. A blue window appears saying **"Windows protected your PC"**
2. Click **More info**
3. Click **Run anyway**

This is normal for apps that are sideloaded. The app is safe to use.

## How to Use the App

The Synapse Ai desktop app works exactly like the web version at [https://synapse.biogreenwax.com](https://synapse.biogreenwax.com) — but in its own dedicated window without browser tabs or URL bars.

### Logging In

1. Launch the app — you'll see the Synapse Ai login page
2. Enter your company email and password
3. Click **Sign In**
4. The app remembers your login, so you won't need to sign in every time

### Navigation

- Use the **sidebar** on the left to navigate between pages
- Click any menu item: Dashboard, Ask Eva, CRM, Calendar, My Profile, etc.
- The sidebar can be collapsed by clicking the arrow icon at the bottom

### Key Features

| Feature | How to Access | Description |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| **Dashboard** | Click "Dashboard" in the sidebar | Overview of your tasks, activities, and follow-ups |
| **Ask Eva** | Click "Ask Eva" in the sidebar | Chat with Eva AI to plan your day, create tasks, and get help |
| **CRM** | Click "CRM" in the sidebar | Manage your leads, clients, and sales pipeline |
| **Workspace** | Click "Workspace" in the sidebar | Organize your workstreams and projects |
| **Email Drafter** | Click "Email Drafter" in the sidebar | Generate professional emails with AI assistance |
| **Calendar** | Click "Calendar" in the sidebar | View your weekly schedule and events |
| **Holidays** | Click "Holidays" in the sidebar | Check company holidays and public holidays |
| **Today's Activity** | Click "Today's Activity" in the sidebar | Log and review your daily work activities |
| **My Profile** | Click "My Profile" in the sidebar | Update your personal and professional details |
| **Notifications** | Click the bell icon at the bottom of the sidebar | View alerts and important updates |

### System Tray

- When you close the app window, it **minimizes to the system tray** (small icon area near the clock)
- To reopen: **double-click** the Synapse Ai icon in the system tray
- To fully exit: **right-click** the tray icon and select **Quit**

### Keyboard Shortcuts

Standard Windows shortcuts work inside the app:

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|

| `Ctrl + V` | Paste |

## Troubleshooting

### "Windows protected your PC" warning
- Click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (see the SmartScreen section above)

### App shows a blank screen or error
- Check your internet connection
- Press `Ctrl + R` to refresh
- Close the app from the system tray and reopen it

### App won't start
- Make sure you extracted the ZIP file before running (don't run it from inside the ZIP)
- Try running as Administrator: right-click **SynapseAi.exe** > **Run as administrator**

### Forgot your password?
- Contact your admin to reset your password

### How to check if the app is running
- Look for the Synapse Ai icon in the system tray (near the clock, bottom-right of your screen)
- Click the **^** arrow to show hidden tray icons if you don't see it
